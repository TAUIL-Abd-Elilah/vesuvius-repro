# Physical probability-bridge split: preregistration

Status: **unscored protocol with execution amendment 01; no bridge-split physical outcomes inspected**.

Execution amendment 01 is a memory-bounded, block-at-a-time refactor published after the original
all-block implementation raised `MemoryError` during development mask construction and before
physical scoring began. It changes object lifetime and label-plane I/O order only. The frozen
scientific protocol below is unchanged; see `PHYSICAL_BRIDGE_SPLIT_AMENDMENT_01.md` and the bound
pre-outcome failure receipt.

## Question

Can a probability-aware, removal-only topology stage split low-confidence mergers between
persistent high-confidence surface regions without sacrificing physical cross-scan recall?

This is not another binary hole filler. Villa already contains `detect_holes.py` and
`fix_holes.py`, including 2-D fill, component-wise closing, alpha wrapping, and merge guards.
The earlier `topology_metric.py` study also showed that ordinary closing/component removal hurts
the easy public set.

## Prior art and narrow scope

This study does **not** claim watershed splitting or topology repair as a new idea.
[`vesuvius-sheet-tools`](https://github.com/IyanDopico/vesuvius-sheet-tools) already separates
surface instances with EDT watershed and splits fused stacks with CLAHE CT intensity, structure
tensors, and intensity watershed. Its public
[#1 evaluation thread](https://github.com/IyanDopico/vesuvius-sheet-tools/issues/1) also records
the important negative prior from the #191 diagnostic: at the tightest contacts, about 78% of
released-model probability profiles have one broad peak, only about 0.5% are separable, and direct
probability carving hurt topology. `vesuvius-unmerge` addresses a related instance-label problem
with continuity-seeded random walks.

The narrower unresolved question here is whether the probability field still contains a useful
subset of *weak* bridges: one low-threshold component, at least two persistent high-confidence
cores, and a complete low-confidence watershed seam that passes strict structural guards. Fully
fused contacts are explicitly out of scope. The contribution is a prospective two-scroll physical
test with an untouched holdout, not a claim to supersede the CT/intensity or continuity methods.

## Frozen source and wait condition

The only real inputs are the 64 `corrected_pmax_l1` arrays generated by the already-public
released-baseline comparison at commit `c62fd475b6f7df716e828b3a774e304e7cf43176` and manifest
content SHA-256 `567a18faa1c8ca7e743c9240133f4200e67e3085823dd4795c4518e3e0e65ac0`.
The baseline is the same array with strict threshold `> 0.2`.

No development or holdout score may run until `inference_completion_receipt.json` binds all 64
arrays, final receipts, and cleanup receipts to the source protocol lock
`97368b402aa313ad6f24442671da0182302530d1ecb0479b0e40d174d855d8b9`. Partial inference
metrics are forbidden.

## Operator

For each low-threshold connected component:

1. Label persistent regions above a frozen persistence threshold.
2. Retain only regions containing at least the frozen number of voxels above the higher seed
   threshold.
3. When at least two protected regions occur, run a deterministic marker watershed on negative
   probability inside the original component.
4. Propose only watershed-line voxels at or below the frozen cut ceiling.
5. Commit the proposal only if every protected marker lands in a distinct output component,
   there are no extra or undersized fragments, no protected voxel is removed, and the edit
   budget is respected. Otherwise leave the whole component unchanged.

The output is always a subset of the fixed-threshold baseline. It cannot create a merger or add
foreground. `editable_mask` prevents writes outside a caller-owned core, and a filtered seam is
accepted only if the complete structural contract still passes.

## Synthetic contracts before real scoring

The public test suite must pass before lock:

- empty and single-sheet inputs are unchanged;
- a weak bridge between two high-confidence regions is split;
- a persistent bridge is unchanged;
- a seam above the cut ceiling is unchanged;
- excluding the seam from the editable core fails closed;
- tiny seeds and over-budget edits fail closed;
- no foreground is added and no persistent voxel is removed;
- contained crop/padding and repeated execution are deterministic;
- NaN, out-of-range values, invalid threshold order, and shape mismatch are rejected;
- the probability-ranking control has exactly the candidate's voxel count with a frozen tie rule;
- all 64 arrays, receipts, cleanup receipts, normalization proofs, label archives, store metadata,
  implementation hashes, and runtime versions must verify before scoring.

## Frozen stratified split

Within each of the eight `(scroll, z_stratum)` groups, rank the eight source blocks by
`SHA256("physical-probability-bridge-split-v1-2026-08-11" | block_id)`. The first four are
development and the last four are holdout. This yields 32 development and 32 holdout blocks,
balanced 16/16 by scroll and 4/4 within every z stratum. Exact block IDs and ranks are recorded
in the protocol lock.

## Development grid

Only these three arms exist; all use low threshold `0.2` and 26-connectivity:

| arm | persistence | seed | cut ceiling | min seed | min output | max removed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `conservative_p45_s70` | 0.45 | 0.70 | 0.30 | 128 | 512 | 0.2% |
| `balanced_p40_s65` | 0.40 | 0.65 | 0.35 | 128 | 512 | 0.5% |
| `sensitive_p35_s60` | 0.35 | 0.60 | 0.30 | 64 | 256 | 1.0% |

All three are scored on development against the corrected fixed-threshold baseline. A candidate
is eligible only if it edits at least four development blocks and at least one per scroll, has
strictly positive pooled mean point-skill delta, nonnegative mean point-skill delta on each
scroll, mean far-37-fraction delta at most `+0.002` on each scroll, and mean recall-37 delta at
least `-0.002` on each scroll.

The exact same requirements must also hold against a per-block matched-budget control. That
control keeps exactly the candidate output's voxel count by descending probability rank over the
complete metric-support extent; increasing C-order flat index breaks probability ties. It uses no
labels. This is deliberately strong: a candidate does not advance merely because deleting the
same amount of generic low-confidence mass would improve the score.

Eligible candidates are ranked, in order, by largest minimum pooled point-skill mean across the
fixed and matched controls, largest matched-control point-skill mean, smallest summed far-37 delta
against fixed, fewest removed voxels, then name. If none is eligible, the study closes negative
and holdout is never scored.

## One-shot holdout gate

Only the selected development candidate is scored on holdout. Success is the conjunction of:

- at least four edited blocks overall and at least one on each scroll;
- the equal-weight scroll-by-z-stratum bootstrap 95% CI lower bound for point-skill delta is
  strictly positive;
- mean point-skill delta is strictly positive on each scroll;
- mean far-37-fraction delta is at most `+0.002` on each scroll;
- mean recall-37 delta is at least `-0.002` on each scroll;
- all 16 holdout blocks on each scroll have the expected point metric denominator;
- every point-skill, per-scroll, far-37, recall-37, and denominator condition above also passes
  against the exact-mass probability-ranking control.

Bootstrap seed is `20260812`, with 10,000 draws. Arc recall/fully-missed changes and visual
overlays are secondary diagnostics and cannot rescue a failed primary conjunction. The invalid
3-D cavity-fill “hole fraction” from the earlier negative study is not used.

## Interpretation limits

A positive result supports this operator on two physical cross-scan label volumes and this
released m7 probability field. It is not evidence that every high-threshold fragment is a
separate sheet, that all mergers are fixed, or that the method generalizes to other models.
A structural component split plus better physical point placement is still not an instance-level
topology proof; no sheet identity exists in these labels. A negative or no-edit result is
published as such, and thresholds are not changed after seeing it.
